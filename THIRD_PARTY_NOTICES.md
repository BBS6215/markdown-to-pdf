In particular, `jschardet` is LGPL-2.1-or-later; its use is limited to input encoding detection, and upstream source is available at [aadsm/jschardet](https://github.com/aadsm/jschardet). Framework, editor, Markdown, icon and document-generation dependencies retain their respective upstream notices. No CJK font files are included.
